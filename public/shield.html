Shield is an early-stage initiative, not a currently available managed security service. Capabilities described as planned are not yet operational or independently validated.

The challenge

Small businesses deserve a recovery plan, not just a warning.

A cyber incident can interrupt sales, customer access, inventory workflows, and the systems a shop relies on every day. Many independent businesses do not have a dedicated security team or a tested recovery process.

01 / DISRUPTION

Operations go offline

Unavailable websites, compromised accounts, or damaged systems can quickly interrupt customer service and sales.

02 / UNCERTAINTY

Recovery is difficult

Owners may not know which systems are safe, which backups can be trusted, or what should be restored first.

03 / LIMITED RESOURCES

Specialist help is costly

Smaller teams need practical safeguards and a recovery approach designed around the realities of independent business.

Built alongside the platform

Security that understands the business.

Shield is intended to work with the same platform components that support a merchant's digital operations—not as an unrelated security dashboard.

  • Merchant identity: protect account access and privileged operations.
  • Storefronts: preserve a path to restore customer-facing services.
  • Stockroom: protect catalog, inventory, and order continuity.
  • Cloud runtime: isolate workloads and constrain resource use.
  • Snapshots: support recovery from verified, known-good state.
Initial controls in development

Security foundations already being integrated

  • Protected node control: admin-gated node management and unique node credentials.
  • Credential hygiene: store node credential digests rather than plaintext tokens.
  • Workload limits: Linux cgroup-backed CPU, memory, and process limits for node containers.
  • Reduced privilege: drop unnecessary container capabilities and enable no-new-privileges.

These are foundational controls, not a complete security program or proof of per-merchant isolation.

Development roadmap

Building toward dependable business continuity.

We are separating implemented safeguards from capabilities that still need engineering, testing, and operational validation.

In progress

Identity and access hardening

Per-node credentials, protected administrative actions, credential rotation, and stronger audit coverage.

Planned

Workload isolation

Per-account containers, cgroups, namespaces, storage boundaries, and fail-closed network policy.

Planned

Threat monitoring

Structured security events, service health signals, alerting, and incident timelines.

Planned

Protected recovery

Backup integrity checks, immutable recovery points, and guarded restoration workflows.

Planned

Clean service restoration

Rebuild affected services in a clean environment and validate them before routing customers back.

Planned

Recovery exercises

Repeatable tests that measure whether a business can restore its essential services and data.
